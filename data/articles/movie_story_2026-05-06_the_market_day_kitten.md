# The Market Day Kitten

Judy Hopps and Nick Wilde were walking through a busy market in Zootopia. The air smelled like fruit and fresh bread. Small mice carried boxes, and tall giraffes looked at clothes from street shops. Judy liked the happy sounds of the city, and Nick carried a paper cup of cold juice.

"It looks like a quiet day," Judy said.

Nick smiled. "You said that last time, and then a goat stole ten pies."

Judy laughed, but then she heard a soft cry near a fruit stand. A little cat from Little Rodentia was standing by a basket of oranges. The kitten looked scared and wet.

Judy bent down. "Hello. Are you lost?"

The kitten nodded. "I came here with my mother," he said. "Then I saw a blue bird and followed it. Now I cannot find her."

Judy looked around the market. There were many feet, many voices, and many streets. The kitten was very small, so it was easy to understand the problem.

"Do not worry," Judy said. "We will help you."

Nick looked at the kitten and then at the ground. "Wait," he said. "Look at his shoes."

The kitten had yellow dust on both shoes. Judy looked surprised.

Nick pointed across the market. "That dust comes from the flower street. They sell yellow pollen flowers there every morning."

So Judy, Nick, and the kitten walked quickly to flower street. On the way, Judy asked shop owners if they had seen a worried cat mother. One rabbit baker said, "Yes, she ran that way a minute ago."

When they reached the flower street, they saw a gray cat looking behind every table. Her eyes were full of worry.

"Mom!" the kitten shouted.

The mother turned and ran to him. She held him close and said, "I was so afraid."

Judy smiled. Nick gave the kitten the rest of his juice.

"Thank you," the mother said. "You were very kind."

As they walked away, Judy looked happy. "A quiet day can still matter," she said.

Nick nodded. "Yes. You do not always need a big case to do something good."

That day, they learned that careful eyes and calm help can bring people home.
