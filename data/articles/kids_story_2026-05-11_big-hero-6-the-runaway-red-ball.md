# Big Hero 6 and the Runaway Red Ball

Hiro was testing a tiny robot in the park. Baymax stood beside him, watching carefully.

"This robot can carry small things," Hiro said. "It will help people at home."

The robot had little wheels and a silver arm. It picked up a leaf, a pencil, and a paper cup.

"Good job," Hiro said.

Then a red ball rolled across the grass. A little boy ran after it, but the ball bounced down a hill.

"My ball!" the boy cried.

The ball rolled faster and faster. It passed a bench. It passed a flower bed. Then it went straight toward the street.

Hiro gasped. "We need to stop it."

Baymax walked after the ball, but he was too slow.

Hiro looked at his tiny robot. "Can you help?"

He pressed a button. The robot zipped across the grass. Its little wheels spun quickly. It reached the ball just before the street and opened its silver arm.

Tap!

The robot stopped the ball.

The little boy clapped. "Wow!"

Baymax picked up the ball and gave it back to him. "Your ball is safe," Baymax said.

"Thank you," the boy said. "Your robot is small, but it is fast."

Hiro smiled. "Small tools can do big jobs."

Just then, the robot picked up a flower by mistake and gave it to Baymax.

Baymax held the flower in one soft hand. "I am now improved with decoration," he said.

Hiro laughed, and the little boy laughed too.

That day, Hiro learned that an invention does not need to be huge to help someone. A good idea, used at the right time, can save the day.
