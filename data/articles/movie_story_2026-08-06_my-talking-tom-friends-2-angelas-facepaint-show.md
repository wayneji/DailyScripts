# My Talking Tom Friends 2: Angela's Facepaint Show

## Part 1: Too Much to Paint

Angela planned a facepaint show for the neighbourhood art afternoon. She prepared four designs: a blue cloud, a green leaf, an orange sun, and a silver moon. Tom, Hank, Becca, Ben, Ginger, and Angela would wear the designs on a small stage at four o'clock.

Angela tested a detailed cloud on Hank. It took seven minutes because he kept turning towards Becca's music. When Ben checked the time, only thirty minutes remained before the show.

"Six detailed faces will take too long," Ben said.

Tom suggested painting faster, but Angela shook her head. Working quickly near someone's eyes was not safe. She needed a plan that saved time without making the facepaint untidy.

## Part 2: Three Helpful Stations

Becca suggested simpler designs: one large shape with two small dots. Angela drew new sample cards, and the friends organised three stations.

Tom helped everyone wash and dry at the first station. Becca helped them choose a design and clipped its card to a board at the second. Angela painted while Ben used a timer at the third. Hank promised to stand still until the bell.

During the test, Ginger chose the silver moon. Its pale shape almost disappeared under the stage lamp. Moving the lamp did not help, so Angela added a thin dark-purple outline. The moon became clear without a new design.

They tested once more, and the three stations worked smoothly.

## Part 3: Ready for the Stage

The line began to move. Tom kept his station clean, Becca found each design card, and Ben announced when three minutes had passed.

After Hank received an orange sun, he heard stage music and started to dance. Tom pointed to the timer, so Hank waited until the paint was dry.

Just before four o'clock, Ginger felt nervous about the silver moon. Angela showed him its clear purple outline in a mirror and offered another design. Ginger kept the moon because he had helped improve it.

All six friends reached the stage on time with bright, clear facepaint. Angela thanked everyone. They had succeeded by sharing the work, testing their plan, and changing a detail instead of rushing.

## Useful A2 Words

- **detailed**: containing many small parts.
- **outline**: a line around the outside edge of a shape.
- **station**: a place where one part of an activity happens.

## Reading Questions

   - C. Ben had forgotten to bring the paint to the stage.

2. Why did Angela add a dark-purple outline to Ginger's moon?
   - A. Ginger had changed his choice from a cloud to a moon.
   - B. The silver shape was difficult to see under the stage light.
   - C. The timer showed that the moon had taken seven minutes.

3. What did Hank do after Tom pointed to the timer?
   - A. He returned to Angela and chose a green leaf.
   - B. He asked Ben to remove the orange facepaint.
   - C. He waited for the paint to dry before dancing.

4. What does the whole story suggest about solving a time problem?
   - A. Dividing work and testing a simpler plan can protect quality.
   - B. The most skilled person should complete every job alone.
   - C. A group should avoid changing a plan after work has begun.
