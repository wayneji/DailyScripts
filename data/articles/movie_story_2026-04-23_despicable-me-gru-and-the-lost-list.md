# Gru and the Lost List

One morning, Gru stood in the kitchen with a cup of coffee. The three girls were ready for school. Margo had her bag, Edith wore her hat, and Agnes held her toy unicorn.

Gru looked at the table. "Today I will make dinner," he said.

Lucy smiled. "That is a good plan."

Gru had a shopping list in his hand. He needed tomatoes, bread, milk, and bananas. He wanted to make a nice meal for the family because they were all busy this week.

Before he left, Agnes hugged him. "Can we have dessert too?"

Gru smiled a little. "We will see."

At the store, Gru walked fast. He did not like shopping very much. Too many people moved slowly, and too many carts were in his way. A Minion followed him and pushed a small cart.

Gru picked up milk and bread. Then he looked in his coat pocket for the list.

It was gone.

Gru stopped walking. "No, no, no," he said. "This is not good."

The Minion looked up at him and made a small sad sound.

Gru tried to remember the food on the list, but he was not sure. Was it apples? Or bananas? Was it cheese? Or tomatoes? He felt angry because he wanted to finish quickly.

Then he took a deep breath. "All right," he said. "We think. We stay calm."

He remembered breakfast. Lucy drank milk. Agnes asked for bananas. Margo said there were no tomatoes at home. So the list was starting to come back to him.

The Minion pointed at a bakery. Gru remembered the bread. After that, he saw a cold box with yogurt and suddenly laughed.

"No, not yogurt," Gru said. "Tomatoes."

Soon the cart was full. On the way out, Gru also bought small ice cream cups for the girls.

At home, the dinner was simple, but everyone liked it. Agnes was very happy about the dessert.

Gru sat at the table and felt proud. He learned that when a plan goes wrong, it is better to stop, think, and try again than to become angry.
