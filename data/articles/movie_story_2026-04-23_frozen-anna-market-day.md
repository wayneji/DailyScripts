# Anna's Market Day

Anna woke up early in Arendelle. The sun was bright, and the streets were busy. Today was market day, and she wanted to buy fresh bread, apples, and flowers for the castle.

She put on her blue dress and walked to the town square. Many people were there. Some people sold fish, and some people sold warm soup. Children ran between the shops and laughed. Anna smiled because the town looked happy.

At the flower shop, Anna met Olaf. He had a small basket in his hands.

"Hello, Anna!" Olaf said. "I want to help you today."

Anna laughed. "Thank you, Olaf. I need flowers for the dining room."

Olaf looked at the flowers with great care. He liked the white ones because they looked like snow. Anna chose pink and yellow flowers because they made her think of spring.

Then Anna looked into her bag. Her face changed.

"Oh no," she said. "My money is not here."

Olaf blinked. "That is a problem."

Anna felt worried. She thought she lost her little purse on the road from the castle. She did not want to go home with nothing. She also did not want to tell the shop people, "Sorry, I cannot pay."

Olaf said, "Let us look for it together."

So Anna and Olaf walked back through the square. They looked near the bread shop, under a bench, and beside a cart with cheese. Anna asked some people, but nobody saw the purse.

After that, a small girl ran to Anna.

"Princess Anna," the girl said, "is this yours?"

In her hand was the little purse.

Anna felt so happy. "Yes, it is! Thank you very much."

The girl smiled. "It was near the apple shop."

Anna bought the bread, apples, and flowers. She also bought one sweet bun for the girl and one carrot for Olaf.

When Anna walked back to the castle, she felt calm again. She learned that a busy day can bring a small problem, but kind people can help make it right.
