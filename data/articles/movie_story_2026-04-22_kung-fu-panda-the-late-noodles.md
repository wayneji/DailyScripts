# Kung Fu Panda and the Late Noodles

One afternoon, Po was helping Mr. Ping at the noodle shop. The sun was bright, and many people were walking in the street. Mr. Ping wanted to send two hot bowls of noodles to the Jade Palace for Tigress and Shifu.

"Po," said Mr. Ping, "please take these noodles now. They must stay warm."

Po stood tall and smiled. "Do not worry, Dad. I am fast."

Mr. Ping looked at him carefully. "Be fast, but be careful too."

Po carried the food in a wooden box with a lid. At first, everything went well. He walked up the road and even started a little song. He was proud because this looked like an easy job.

Then he heard a child crying near a market stand. Po stopped and looked around. A little rabbit was standing alone by some baskets of apples.

"What is wrong?" Po asked.

The child said, "I cannot find my mother."

Po wanted to help. He looked left and right and saw a woman running between the stands. She was calling for her child. Po waved both arms and shouted, "Over here!"

The mother came quickly and thanked him many times. Po smiled because he felt good. But then he remembered the noodles.

"Oh no," he said.

He opened the wooden box. The bowls were still safe, but the noodles were not very warm now. Po started to run up the mountain path. He ran faster and faster, but when he reached the Jade Palace, he was breathing hard.

Tigress opened the box and touched one bowl.

"These are late," she said.

Po looked down. "I know. I stopped to help a lost child."

Shifu came over and listened. He was quiet for a moment.

"Helping others is important," Shifu said. "But when you have two duties, you must think clearly and act wisely."

Po felt bad, but Tigress was not angry. "Next time, ask someone nearby to help too," she said.

Po nodded. "Yes. I can be kind and smart together."

Later, Po went back to the shop. He told Mr. Ping the whole story. Mr. Ping gave him fresh noodles and said, "Then go again, my son. This time, take the shorter road."

Po laughed, took the new box, and delivered it on time.

That day, Po learned that a big heart is good, but careful thinking makes help even better.
