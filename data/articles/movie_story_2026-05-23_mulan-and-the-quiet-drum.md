# Mulan and the Quiet Drum

Mulan woke early in the small camp near the mountains. The air was cool, and the sky was pale blue. Today was important. The soldiers would march at noon, and Mulan had one job: she had to bring the signal drum to the front gate.

The drum was not big, but it was very special. It helped the team move together. Mulan tied the drum to her bag and started walking. Then she heard a loud crack. A rope on the drum bag broke, and the drum rolled under a cart.

Mulan looked around. The camp was busy. People were carrying water, shoes, and food. She wanted to fix the problem fast, but the drum was heavy, and the cart could not move.

She saw a young soldier named Ling nearby. He was trying to carry three spears at once, and he looked worried.

“Can you help me?” Mulan asked.

Ling stopped and smiled. “Yes. I can hold the cart.”

Together they lifted one side of the cart. Mulan reached under it and pulled out the drum. It had a small scratch, but it was still strong. Then Ling gave her a long strip of cloth from his sleeve. Mulan tied the drum again, but this time she made two knots.

“Good idea,” Ling said.

Mulan nodded. “The drum is safe now.”

They carried it to the front gate just in time. When the leader struck the drum, the sound was clear and strong. The soldiers lined up and marched with steady steps. Mulan felt proud, not because she did everything alone, but because she asked for help and worked with a friend.

That evening, the camp was quiet. The mountains were dark, and the stars were small and bright. Mulan sat near the fire and looked at the drum.

She learned a simple lesson that day: a brave heart is important, but trust and teamwork can make brave hearts even stronger.
