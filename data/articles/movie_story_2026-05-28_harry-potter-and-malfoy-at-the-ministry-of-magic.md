# Harry Potter and Malfoy at the Ministry of Magic

## Part 1: A Busy Place

Harry Potter went to the Ministry of Magic with Ron and Hermione. The building was large and full of people. Doors opened and closed. Papers moved on desks. Harry had one simple job. He needed to take a box of old files to a safe room.

Then he saw Draco Malfoy. Draco was there for work too. He looked at Harry and said, “You again?”

Harry stayed calm. “I am only here for the files.”

Hermione looked at both boys. “This is a place for work,” she said. “Not for fighting.”

Harry wanted to finish fast and go home. But when he picked up the box, he saw that one paper was missing. It had fallen under a table near the lift.

He bent down, but the lift door opened. People came out, and the hall became crowded. Before Harry could speak, Draco saw the problem.

“Wait,” Draco said. “Do not step there.”

## Part 2: The Missing Paper

Harry found the paper and pulled it out. It was old and had a gold seal. Hermione read it and said, “This is important. It has names and room numbers.”

Harry nodded. “Then we must return it now.”

Draco took the note. “I know the records room,” he said. “Follow me.”

Harry was surprised, but he followed. The Ministry felt like a maze. There were many halls and many doors. They turned left, then right, then down a narrow stair. On the way, a small box of stamps fell from a shelf. The stamps rolled over the floor.

Ron laughed. Even Draco gave a short smile.

Harry picked up the stamps, and Hermione counted them. “All here,” she said.

At last, they reached the records room. An old wizard was waiting there. He looked worried until Harry gave him the paper.

“Oh, thank you,” the man said. “I thought it was lost.”

Draco said, “It is safe now.”

## Part 3: A Small Lesson

The old wizard gave each of them a chocolate coin. Ron was very happy. Hermione said thank you. Harry put his coin in his pocket and smiled.

On the way out, Draco walked next to Harry.

“You did well,” Draco said.

Harry looked at him. “So did you.”

Draco looked a little shy. “I do not say this often,” he said, “but this was good work.”

Harry laughed. “That is okay.”

When they reached the lift, Hermione said, “See? Work is easier when people stay calm.” Ron nodded.

Harry thought about the day. It was not a big battle. It was only a small problem in a busy place. But he learned something important. Brave people do not always fight. Sometimes they listen, help, and stay kind.

At the Ministry of Magic, Harry and Draco did not become close friends. But they did work together. And that was a good start.
