# Moana and the Basket on the Beach

One morning, Moana walked along the beach of Motunui. The sea was bright blue, and small waves touched the sand. She wanted to bring fruit to her grandmother’s old cooking place near the trees.

Moana carried a big basket with bananas, coconuts, and sweet green leaves. It was a little heavy, but she was happy. She liked helping her family.

When she reached the beach path, a strong wind came from the sea. The basket fell from her hands and rolled down the sand. Some bananas fell out, and the leaves flew into the air.

"Oh no!" Moana cried, and she ran after them.

At that moment, Hei Hei walked near the basket and looked at it in his strange, silly way. He stepped on one leaf and made everything worse.

Moana picked up two bananas, but three coconuts rolled closer to the water. She was worried because the tide was coming in. If the food went into the sea, her family could not use it.

Then Maui came down the path. He saw Moana running, the coconuts moving, and Hei Hei standing on the basket.

Maui crossed his arms. "You really know how to make a normal morning exciting."

Moana gave him a quick look. "Very funny. Help me!"

Maui ran to catch the coconuts. He was fast, but one coconut rolled between his feet, and he almost fell. Moana laughed, even though she was busy. Together they picked up the fruit and the leaves.

Then Maui lifted the basket and said, "This thing is too open. The wind can take everything."

Moana looked around and found some long plant strings near the trees. She used them to tie the top of the basket. Maui added large leaves over the fruit, so nothing could fly away.

"This is better," Moana said.

They carried the basket together to the cooking place. Her family was glad to see the food was safe.

Later, Moana thanked Maui. "You helped a lot."

Maui smiled. "Of course. I’m amazing. But you had the smart idea."

Moana looked at the sea and felt proud. It was only a small problem, but she solved it with calm thinking and help from a friend.

That day, Moana learned that even simple work needs care, and Maui learned that helping with small things can be important too.
