# The Incredible Family Day

The Parr family is very special. They all have superpowers! Bob is super strong, Helen can stretch like rubber, Violet can become invisible, Dash is very fast, and baby Jack-Jack can do many amazing things. Life in their house is always busy.

One Saturday, they want to go to the park for a picnic. But, there is a big problem. A giant robot appears in the city center. It is destroying the buildings and the cars. People are running away because they are afraid. The family looks at each other. They know they have to help.

Bob runs to the robot and tries to stop it with his strength. Helen stretches and grabs the robot's legs. Violet uses her invisibility to sneak behind the robot and turn off its power button. Dash runs around so fast that the robot gets dizzy. Baby Jack-Jack even turns into a fireball and makes the robot stop!

Together, the family stops the robot. The city is safe again. Everyone is happy. They go back to the park for their picnic. They eat sandwiches and laugh. They learn that being a hero is good, but being a family that works together is better. They are happy to have each other.
