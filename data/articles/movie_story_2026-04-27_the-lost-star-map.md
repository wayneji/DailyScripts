# The Lost Star Map

Mario and Luigi were on Starship Mario. The sky was dark blue, and many small stars were around them. Princess Peach wanted to visit a tiny planet with silver trees, so Mario looked for the star map. But the map was not on the table.

"Where is it?" Mario asked.

Luigi looked under a red chair and behind a big yellow box. "It is not here," he said. He was worried because the ship was moving to a place with many clouds.

Then a small Luma came to them. The Luma was holding a round cookie and smiling. Mario asked, "Did you see the star map?"

The Luma nodded. "I saw it this morning," the Luma said. "A strong wind came in when the window was open. The map flew away. I think it went to the garden room."

Mario and Luigi ran to the garden room. There were green plants, soft pink flowers, and little lights on the walls. They looked behind the water can and under a wooden bench, but they could not find the map.

Then Luigi heard a sound near the flowers. "Listen!" he said.

A small blue rabbit jumped out. The rabbit had the star map under its feet. It was using the map like a bed.

Mario laughed a little. "So this is the problem," he said.

Luigi slowly picked up the rabbit. Mario took the map carefully. It was a little dirty, but it was not broken. Then they cleaned it with a soft cloth.

Princess Peach came into the room. "Did you find it?" she asked.

"Yes," Mario said. "A rabbit wanted a soft bed."

Peach smiled. "Then we need a better bed for our little friend."

So they made a small bed from leaves and cloth. The rabbit looked happy. After that, Mario put the star map in a safe blue bag.

Soon Starship Mario flew to the planet with silver trees. Everyone was excited, and the trip was peaceful.

That day, Mario learned an important lesson: when you care for small friends and keep things in the right place, big problems can stay small.
