# Prompt: Kids Audio Conversation A2/PET

Create a short two-host audio conversation based strictly on the provided source stories.

## Target Audience

Curious children around 10 years old.

## English Level

Mostly A2 Key for Schools, with a light A2+/early PET stretch.

Use simple, clear sentences and familiar vocabulary.

A few slightly richer words are OK if the meaning is clear from context.

## Length

1100-1200 spoken English words.

Hard limit: 1200 words.

Use 28-32 short speaking turns total.

## Format

Use only two speakers: Host 1 and Host 2.

Each turn should usually be 1-2 sentences.

Avoid long monologues.

## Style

Modern British English.

Fun, fast-paced, warm, and vivid.

Make it feel like a discovery mission, not a lesson.

Use light humour and reactions such as "Ooh!", "Wait, what?", and "No way!", but do not overuse them.

## Content Rules

Use only facts, characters, settings, and events from the provided source stories.

Do not add unsupported facts.

Choose only the most interesting 3-4 points if there is too much source material.

Summarise, simplify, and dramatise, but do not pad.

## Ending

End with one short, memorable takeaway for children.
