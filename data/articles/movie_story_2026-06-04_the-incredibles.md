# The Incredibles and The Missing Morning Bell

## Part 1: A Strange Quiet Morning

Violet woke up early because she wanted a calm morning before school. But when she went into the kitchen, Dash was already there. He was not eating. He was looking out the window with big eyes.

"Something is wrong," Dash said. "The school bell did not ring."

Violet listened. He was right. Every morning, the bell from the school on the hill made a loud sound. Today there was only quiet air and a few birds.

Mrs. Incredible put pancakes on the table. "No hero work before breakfast," she said.

Mr. Incredible smiled. "Maybe the bell is just broken."

Dash shook his head. "It rang yesterday. I know that sound."

Violet looked at the hill again. She saw one small flash near the school roof. It was there for only a second.

"Mom, can Dash and I go and check after breakfast?" Violet asked.

Helen thought for a moment. "Only if you stay careful and call us if there is real trouble."

The two children ran outside after breakfast. Dash wanted to race at once, but Violet stopped him.

"Slow down," she said. "If we want answers, we must look first."

When they reached the school yard, they found the doors open but the place was empty. The bell rope in the hall was cut. On the floor there was silver dust and one tiny wheel.

"This is not normal," Violet said softly.

Dash picked up the wheel. "It looks like it came from a toy car."

Then they heard a low metal sound from the roof.

## Part 2: The Roof Robot

Dash ran up the stairs, and Violet followed him. On the roof they saw a little robot with long arms and a square head. It was pulling wires out of the school bell machine and dropping them into a bag.

"Stop!" Dash shouted.

The robot jumped and rolled fast across the roof on four tiny wheels. Violet made a force field in front of it. The robot hit the blue wall and fell back with a clank.

For one second, Dash smiled. Then the robot opened its chest and sent out three smaller flying bots.

"That is not fair," Dash said.

One bot flew near Violet's face, so she turned invisible and moved to the left. Another bot tried to grab Dash's shoe, but he was too quick. The third bot lifted the bag and started to fly away.

"The bag has the bell parts!" Violet called.

Dash ran in a red line across the roof and caught the bag before the bot could rise higher. But the big robot used its long arms to grab a pipe and swing toward the edge of the building.

Violet knew it would escape if they only chased it. She looked at the loose wires, the bell machine, and the metal rain pipe near the robot.

"Dash, bring me the silver dust!" she said.

Dash was back in one second. Violet threw the dust into the air with a small force push. The dust covered the robot's wheels and the wet pipe under it.

The robot slipped at once. Its wheels spun, its arms waved, and then it fell into Violet's soft force field bubble.

Inside the bubble, the robot made one sad beeping sound.

Dash looked inside its open chest. "There is a note!"

The note said: `TOO NOISY. NEED QUIET TO STUDY.`

## Part 3: A Better Answer

Violet and Dash looked at each other. This was not a supervillain plan. It was a machine trying to solve a problem in a very bad way.

"Maybe someone built it for a science project," Violet said.

Soon a shy boy from the next street came onto the roof. He was carrying a remote control and breathing hard.

"I am sorry," he said. "My name is Owen. I made Study-Bot. I have a big test today. The bell always rings while I study in the early morning. I told it to find quiet, but I did not say how."

Dash frowned. "So it stole the bell?"

Owen looked down. "I only wanted one peaceful hour."

Violet called her parents, and soon Mr. Incredible and Mrs. Incredible arrived. Mr. Incredible fixed the heavy bell machine and put the parts back. Helen spoke kindly to Owen.

"Good inventions need good rules," she said.

Owen nodded. "I understand."

Then Dash had an idea. "What if the school lets you study in the library before classes? It is quiet there."

Owen's face became bright. "That would help a lot."

The principal came up to the roof just as the bell started working again. He listened to the whole story and agreed to open the library early for students who wanted a calm place.

At last, the morning bell rang across the hill. It was loud, clear, and friendly.

Dash covered his ears and laughed. "Okay, maybe a little too friendly."

Violet smiled. They had solved the problem without a big fight. On the walk home, Dash bounced the tiny robot wheel in his hand.

"Tomorrow," he said, "I hope breakfast is normal."

Violet laughed. "With this family? Never."

## Useful A2 Words

- **quiet**: with little or no noise.
- **rope**: a strong thick string.
- **principal**: the head teacher of a school.
