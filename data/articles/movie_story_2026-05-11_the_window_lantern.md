# The Window Lantern

Mira woke up before breakfast because rain was tapping on the roof. The little village below the hill looked gray and quiet, and most people stayed inside with warm cups of tea.

But Mira was excited. That evening, her class would visit the old lighthouse by the sea. Each student had to bring one small lantern, and Mira had made hers with blue paper and a silver star.

"Keep it safe," her mother said.

"I will," Mira answered.

After lunch, Mira put the lantern on the kitchen table and went to find her rain boots. When she came back, the lantern was not there.

Mira looked under the chairs. She looked beside the door. She even looked in the bread basket, but the lantern was gone.

"Oh no," she said. "The trip starts soon."

Her little brother Leo stood near the window. He looked worried.

"I only wanted to see it in the light," Leo said softly. "Then the wind pushed the window open."

Mira hurried to the window. Outside, the rain was softer now, and a small blue shape was moving near the garden path.

"There it is!" she said.

Mira and Leo ran outside together. The lantern had rolled beside a wet flowerpot. One side was bent, and the silver star was loose.

Leo's eyes filled with tears. "I broke it."

Mira wanted to be angry, but she saw how sorry he was. She took a deep breath.

"We still have time to fix it," she said.

They carried the lantern inside. Leo held the paper flat while Mira added new glue. Her mother found a dry ribbon, and soon the silver star was shining again.

When Mira reached the lighthouse, her lantern was not perfect, but it was bright. Leo stood beside her and smiled proudly.

That evening, Mira learned that accidents can feel big at first, but patience and help can make things bright again.
