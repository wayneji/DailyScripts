# Inside Out: A Rainy Day at School

Riley Andersen sat in her classroom. It was a Tuesday morning, and the sky outside was gray and rainy. She looked out the window and felt a little sad. She missed her old home in Minnesota. At the control panel inside her head, Joy and Sadness looked at each other. They knew Riley was not feeling like herself today.

"Today is going to be a difficult day," Joy said with a sigh. 

Sadness walked slowly to the control panel. "Maybe it is okay to feel a little sad," Sadness said quietly. "Riley is in a new place. It is normal to miss her old friends."

Just then, Riley's teacher asked her a question. "Riley, what is the answer?" Riley did not know. She felt embarrassed and did not want to talk. She looked down at her desk. 

At the control panel, Sadness touched the button. A small tear fell from Riley's eye. The teacher saw this and walked over to her desk. "Are you okay, Riley?" the teacher asked in a kind voice.

Riley looked up. "I miss my old school," she said. 

The teacher smiled. "It is okay to miss your old home. But you will make new friends here. I am sure of it."

After class, a girl named Maya walked up to Riley. "Do you want to play at lunch?" Maya asked. Riley smiled and said yes. 

At the control panel, Joy jumped up and down. "See? I knew something good would happen!" 

Sadness smiled gently. "Sometimes feeling sad helps us find the right words. That is how we connect with others."

Riley learned that it is normal to feel sad sometimes. Sadness is not a bad feeling. It helps us share our hearts with people around us. And sometimes, it leads us to new friends and new happiness.
