# The Missing Garden Glove

Peter Rabbit was in Mr. McGregor's garden early in the morning. The grass was wet, the air was cool, and the sun was just coming up over the trees. Peter wanted to find sweet carrots for his family, but he also wanted to stay out of trouble.

Benjamin Bunny came with him and looked nervous. "We should be quick," Benjamin said.

Peter smiled. "We will be quick and careful."

They moved between the garden rows and found many good vegetables. Then Peter saw something strange near the cabbages.

"Look at that," he said.

A small brown glove was lying in the dirt. It was not Mr. McGregor's glove because it was too small. Peter picked it up and looked around.

Then they heard tiny crying behind a flower pot. A little field mouse came out. She looked very upset.

"Is this your glove?" Peter asked.

The mouse nodded. "Yes," she said. "I lost it while carrying seeds for my family. Now I cannot carry the basket well, and I am late."

Peter saw a small basket near the wall. It was full of seeds, but one handle was broken.

"That is the real problem," Benjamin said.

Peter thought for a moment. Then he took a long piece of plant string from the ground. He tied the glove around the broken basket handle to make it soft and strong.

The little mouse looked surprised. "Will that work?"

"Let us see," Peter said.

Together they lifted the basket. This time, the handle stayed in place. The mouse smiled for the first time that morning.

"Thank you," she said. "Now I can carry it home."

Just then they heard a door open at the house.

"Mr. McGregor!" Benjamin whispered.

Peter quickly gave the basket to the mouse. She ran through a hole in the wall, safe and happy. Then Peter and Benjamin jumped behind the lettuce rows and waited very still.

After a minute, the garden was quiet again.

As they left, Benjamin said, "You always find trouble."

Peter laughed. "Maybe. But today we also helped someone."

That morning, Peter learned that even in a risky place, kindness and quick thinking can do something good.
