# Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire: The Lost Invitation

At Hogwarts, the Great Hall was full of light. The Goblet of Fire stood in the middle like a cold blue lamp, and many students were still talking about the champions. Harry Potter sat with Hermione Granger near the end of the table. Harry felt nervous because the next day would bring new magic and a new task.

Hermione noticed his face. "You need fresh air," she said.

So after dinner they walked into the corridor. The castle was quieter there. Candles burned on the walls, and the windows showed the dark sky. Hermione held a small paper envelope in her hand. It was an invitation to the library study room. She wanted Harry to read with her, but the wind blew hard when they opened a side door.

The envelope flew away.

"Oops," Hermione said. She ran after it.

Harry followed her through two hallways and down a stone stair. The envelope landed near a suit of armor, but a young Ravenclaw girl was standing there too. She looked worried and close to tears.

"I lost my dance card," she said. "I cannot find my friends. I am late for the practice room."

Harry looked at Hermione. Hermione looked at the card on the floor.

"It is here," Harry said gently. He picked it up and gave it to her.

The girl smiled with relief. "Thank you. I was so afraid."

Then Harry and Hermione used the card to help her find the right room. They walked with her to the end of the corridor, where music was coming from behind a door. Her friends waved when they saw her. The girl laughed, and her fear went away.

After that, Hermione found another paper and gave Harry his study notes back. "You see?" she said. "Even on a strange night, small help can matter."

Harry smiled. He still felt nervous about the tasks, but his heart felt lighter. At Hogwarts, he learned that bravery is not only for big battles. It is also for quiet moments, when you stop, help someone, and keep walking together.
