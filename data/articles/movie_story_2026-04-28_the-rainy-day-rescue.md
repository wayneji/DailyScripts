# The Rainy Day Rescue

Mabel lived near a green forest and loved to study animals. She worked with a new machine from the Hoppers team. The machine could help a person see and move like a small animal for a short time. Mabel thought it was exciting, and she wanted to learn more about the world under the trees.

One morning, dark clouds filled the sky. Mabel looked at her watch and said, "I only have one hour before the heavy rain comes." She used the machine and became a small beaver. Her brown tail felt strange at first, but then she smiled. "I can do this," she said.

The forest looked very different from the ground. The grass was tall, the air was cool, and the sounds were louder. Then Mabel heard soft cries near the river.

She ran there and found two baby rabbits. They were on a little piece of land between the water and some rocks. The river was getting bigger because of the coming rain.

"Do not be afraid," Mabel said, but the rabbits were shaking.

Mabel looked around. A broken tree branch was in the mud. She had an idea. Because she was a beaver, she could move wood more easily now. So she pushed and pulled the branch toward the rabbits.

It was hard work. The branch was wet, and the mud was deep. Then the first drops of rain started to fall.

"Come on," Mabel said. She put the branch across the water like a small bridge.

One rabbit crossed first, then the second. They jumped onto safe ground just as the rain became strong.

A fox watched from far away, but it did not come closer because of the storm. Mabel led the rabbits to a dry place under a big tree.

Soon the machine time was almost over. Mabel returned to her normal body and looked at the two rabbits. They were safe and calm now.

She smiled and said, "Today I learned something important. Small bodies can still do big good things."

After that, Mabel wrote everything in her notebook, and she hoped to help the forest again very soon.
