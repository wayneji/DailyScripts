# Zootopia: The Missing Lemon Pie

On a bright morning in Zootopia, Judy Hopps was happy to start her shift. The streets were busy, and the city was full of noise, cars, and small talks. She had one small job before lunch. She needed to bring a lemon pie from a bakery to Chief Bogo for a meeting.

Nick Wilde walked beside her with a relaxed smile. “One pie, one safe trip. Easy,” he said.

Judy held the box with care. “Yes, but I still want to be careful. Bogo is very serious about lunch.”

They walked across the plaza and past the fountain. Then a strong wind came from between two tall buildings. The box shook in Judy’s arms. She stopped, but the lid opened a little. A sweet smell filled the air.

“Oh no,” Judy said. “The pie is gone!”

Inside the box, only a few crumbs were left. The lemon pie had not fallen on the ground. It had been eaten, piece by piece, by a small group of hungry baby mice who were hiding near the bakery door. They looked very nervous.

Nick crouched down. “Looks like we found the real problem.”

Judy took a slow breath. The baby mice said they were waiting for their mother, who worked in the next street. They had not eaten breakfast.

Judy looked at Nick. “We can help them, but we still need a pie for Bogo.”

Nick pointed to the bakery window. “Then let’s solve both problems.”

The baker was kind. He made a new lemon pie very fast, and Judy and Nick carried it to Bogo. After that, they brought warm bread and milk to the baby mice and helped them find their mother.

At the meeting, Bogo looked at the pie, then at Judy. “You were late,” he said.

Judy smiled. “Yes, sir. But everyone got lunch.”

That day, she learned that rules matter, but kindness can also save the day.
