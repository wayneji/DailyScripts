# Finding Nemo and the Bright Blue Button

Nemo was helping his class clean the reef after a windy night. Little pieces of sea grass were everywhere. A shell was upside down. A tiny cup was stuck between two rocks.

Mr. Ray smiled at the class. "Good helpers make the ocean happy," he said.

Nemo swam near the pink coral with his friend Pearl. Suddenly, he saw something bright under a leaf.

"Look!" Nemo said. "It is a blue button."

The button was small and round. It shone in the water like a little moon.

"Maybe it belongs to someone," Pearl said.

Nemo nodded. "We should find the owner."

They asked a crab, but the crab shook his claws. They asked a turtle, but the turtle only laughed and said, "Cool button, little dude."

Nemo did not give up. He carried the button carefully to the school rock. Then he saw a young fish crying beside a sea plant.

"What's wrong?" Nemo asked.

"My bag is broken," the fish said. "One button is gone, and now my lunch keeps floating away."

Nemo looked at the bag. It was yellow, and it had three blue buttons. But one place was empty.

"I think we found it," Nemo said.

The young fish smiled. "That's my button!"

Mr. Ray helped tie the button back on with a thin piece of sea grass. The bag closed again, and the lunch stayed safe inside.

"Thank you," said the young fish.

Nemo felt warm and proud. He was small, but he had solved a real problem.

That afternoon, the reef looked clean and bright. Nemo learned that even a tiny thing can be important to someone, and a careful helper can make a big difference.
