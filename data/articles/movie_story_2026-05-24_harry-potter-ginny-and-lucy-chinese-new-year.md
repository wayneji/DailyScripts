# Harry Potter, Ginny, and Lucy Welcome Chinese New Year

It was a cold evening at Hogwarts, and snow lay on the castle roofs. In Ravenclaw tower, a new student named Lucy looked out of the window and smiled. She missed her home, but she had a bright idea. “This year, I want to share Chinese New Year with everyone,” she said.

Harry Potter and Ginny Weasley wanted to help. Lucy explained the red lanterns, the good wishes, and the joy of a new start. So they decorated a small hall near the library. They hung red paper cutouts, placed shiny lanterns on the tables, and wrote happy messages on long strips of paper. Harry tried to make a paper rabbit, but it looked more like a strange bird. Ginny and Lucy laughed.

Then Draco Malfoy walked by and gave a sharp laugh. “What is all this? School is for magic, not for silly party games,” he said.

Lucy felt hurt, but Harry answered calmly. “It is a festival about family, hope, and good wishes. There is nothing silly about kindness.” Ginny added, “You are welcome to stay if you want to learn.”

Malfoy snorted and left.

That night, the celebration began. Students from many houses came to taste the dumplings and write wishes for the new year. The hall felt warm and peaceful.

Later, Malfoy returned alone. He had heard the happy music and smelled the sweet food. He looked at the bright room and saw that people were sharing, helping, and smiling. Lucy handed him a small red envelope with a note: “May you have peace and good luck.”

Malfoy felt ashamed. The next day, he returned and said, “Your festival was better than I thought.”

Lucy smiled. Harry and Ginny smiled too.

Lucy learned that when people share respect and kindness, good things grow. Malfoy learned another lesson too: good deeds bring good rewards, but unkind words bring sadness back to the person who says them.
