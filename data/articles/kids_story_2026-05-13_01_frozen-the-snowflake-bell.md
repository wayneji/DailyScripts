# Frozen and the Snowflake Bell

Anna was helping Elsa decorate the castle hall for a winter music day. There were blue ribbons on the walls, warm candles on the tables, and little paper snowflakes hanging from the windows.

Olaf stood beside a box of bells. "I am in charge of cheerful ringing," he said proudly.

Elsa smiled. "Please be careful with the silver snowflake bell. It is for the first song."

The bell was small and bright, with a tiny snowflake on its handle. Anna placed it on a blue cloth near the piano. Then she went to help carry chairs.

A few minutes later, Olaf looked at the table. "Um... where is the special bell?"

Anna hurried back. The blue cloth was empty.

"Oh no," she said. "The music day starts soon."

Elsa looked around calmly. "Let us follow the sound."

They all stood still. At first, they heard only footsteps and voices. Then there was a soft jingle from near the window.

Olaf gasped. "The bell is singing without us!"

Anna ran to the window and saw a small bird outside. It had picked up the bell's ribbon and carried it to a snowy branch.

"Maybe it likes music too," Anna said.

Elsa made a gentle snow bridge from the window to the branch. Anna walked across carefully and held out some crumbs from her pocket. The bird dropped the ribbon and pecked at the crumbs.

Anna caught the bell before it fell.

When the first song began, Olaf rang the bell softly. Everyone smiled.

That day, Anna learned that staying calm helps you hear the answer, even when the room feels full of noise.
