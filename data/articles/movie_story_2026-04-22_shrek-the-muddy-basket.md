# Shrek and the Muddy Basket

Shrek had a quiet morning in his swamp. The air was cool, and the birds were singing in the trees. He wanted to take some fresh vegetables to Fiona for lunch. He found a big basket and put in carrots, onions, and some small potatoes from his garden.

Before he left, Donkey ran to him with a happy face.

"Shrek! Where are you going?" he asked.

"To the hill near the old tree," Shrek said. "Fiona is there with a book. I want to bring lunch."

Donkey smiled. "Great! I can help."

They walked down the small path together. The basket was heavy, but Shrek carried it easily. Donkey talked and talked about food, clouds, and songs. Shrek said very little, but he did not look angry.

Then they came to a part of the path with wet ground. It rained the night before, so the road was full of mud. Donkey tried to jump over it, but he landed badly. His back leg hit the basket.

The basket fell into the mud.

"Oh no!" Donkey cried.

The carrots and potatoes were dirty. One onion rolled away under a bush. Shrek looked at the muddy food and gave a long sigh.

"I wanted to give Fiona a nice lunch," he said.

Donkey put his ears down. "I am sorry, Shrek. I wanted to help, but I made a mess."

Shrek was quiet for a moment. Then he said, "Okay. We have a problem, so we fix it."

First, they picked up the food. Donkey found the onion under the bush. Then they went to a small stream near the path. Shrek washed the potatoes and carrots in the clean water. Donkey used large leaves to dry them. After that, Shrek cleaned the basket as well as he could.

When they reached Fiona, she looked at the basket and smiled.

"This lunch had an adventure," she said.

Donkey laughed. "A muddy adventure!"

Fiona washed the vegetables again and made a warm soup for all of them. It smelled very good, and they ate together under the tree.

Shrek felt happy. The lunch was not perfect, but it was still special.

That day, Donkey learned that helping means being careful, and Shrek learned that a simple problem can still end in a good meal with friends.
