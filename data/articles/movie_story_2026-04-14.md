# Moana’s Sunny Morning

The sun was high in the sky over the island of Motunui. Moana was happy because it was a perfect day for sailing. Her boat was ready, and the ocean looked very calm and blue. She wanted to practice sailing to a small island nearby to find some special shells for her grandmother.

She started her journey with excitement. However, after an hour, the weather began to change. Dark clouds appeared in the sky, and the wind started to blow very hard. Her boat moved quickly from side to side. Moana felt a little scared because the waves were getting bigger. She tried to turn the boat, but a rope got stuck. 

"Oh no!" Moana said. She looked around, but she was alone. She remembered what her father told her about keeping calm. She took a deep breath. Instead of worrying, she decided to fix the rope first. She climbed to the front of the boat and used all her strength to pull the stuck rope. It was very difficult, but she did not give up. 

After a few minutes, the rope came free. Moana quickly moved the sail and turned the boat away from the bad weather. The sun came out again, and the sea became quiet. She did not reach the other island, but she made it back to Motunui safely.

When she arrived at the beach, she felt proud of herself. She realized that even when things go wrong, staying calm and trying your best is the most important thing. She did not get the shells today, but she learned how to be a better sailor and how to face her fears. She decided to try again another day when the weather was better. Friendship with the ocean and belief in herself made her smile.
