# Toy Story and the Broken Rocket

Woody found a small blue rocket toy under the bed. It was shiny, but one wing was broken. Buzz Lightyear looked at it and said, “That rocket can still fly. We just need a new part.”

Andy was not in the room, so the toys had time to help. Woody called Jessie, and she brought a tiny piece of red plastic from a toy box. Rex held the rocket still, but he shook because he was nervous. Buzz used a small sticker to cover the crack, and Woody tied the new wing in place with a thin thread.

The rocket looked simple, but it was strong. The toys put it on the shelf and gave it a gentle push. It rolled across the floor and stopped near the window. Then the sun came in and made the blue paint shine.

“Not bad,” said Woody.

“Excellent,” said Buzz.

Soon Andy came back into the room. He saw the rocket and smiled. “You fixed my toy,” he said. He did not know how much teamwork it took, but he was happy.

That night, the toys sat together and felt proud. Woody learned that old things can still be useful when friends work together. Buzz learned that small help can make a big result. The broken rocket was not perfect, but it was ready for another day of play.
