# Zootopia: The Lost Map

Judy Hopps was busy in Zootopia. She was a police rabbit, and she liked to help people. One morning, she saw a little mouse named Pip. Pip looked very sad. He lost his map of the city. He needed to find his school, but he did not know the way.

"Do not worry, Pip," Judy said with a kind smile. "I can help you find your school."

They walked together down the big streets of Zootopia. They passed a tall giraffe and a fast cheetah. Pip was a little scared of the big animals, but Judy walked next to him to keep him safe. Suddenly, they saw something on the ground near a large fountain. It was a folded piece of paper.

"Is that your map?" Judy asked. 

Pip ran to the paper. "Yes! It is!" he shouted. He was very happy.

Judy looked at the map with Pip. They saw that the school was just around the corner. "You are a very brave rabbit, Judy," Pip said. "Thank you for your help."

Judy felt good because she helped a new friend. She learned that being a police officer is not just about catching bad guys. It is also about being kind to everyone. Even a small mouse can be important, and even a big city can feel small when you have a friend by your side. Judy said goodbye to Pip and went back to work. She was ready to help someone else.
