# A2 Key for Schools Writing Part 7: Helping a Lost Dog

![Writing Part 7 picture prompts](./a2_writing_part7_2026-05-28_helping-a-lost-dog-color.jpg)

## Sample Story

Last Friday, Tom and Mia were walking home from school through the park. Suddenly, they saw a small brown dog near a bench. It looked tired and scared. Mia said, "Maybe it is lost." The dog had a collar, but it was thirsty, so Tom gave it some water. Mia looked at the phone number on the collar and called the owner. They waited on the bench and talked to the dog quietly. After ten minutes, a woman ran to the park gate. She was very happy to see her dog again. She thanked Tom and Mia, and they felt proud because they helped someone.

**Word count: 105**
