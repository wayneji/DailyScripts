# Ralph and the Lost Button

Ralph was in the bright, noisy world of the internet. Screens flashed with colors. Small ads popped up. New songs played. Vanellope sat on a chair and watched the moving lights with wide eyes.

They were there to find a small part for Vanellope’s game, but the internet was busy and strange. Ralph liked the big signs and the funny videos. Vanellope liked the fast links and the shiny new things. Still, they stayed together.

Then Vanellope saw a small gold button fall under a table in a game shop. It looked important. A man in a blue jacket was walking away, and the button rolled near his foot. Ralph ran to get it, but he bumped into a stack of boxes. The boxes fell, and the button rolled even farther away.

“Oh no,” Ralph said. “I made it worse.”

Vanellope was calm. “It is okay. Let’s look slowly.”

They went under the table. It was dark and full of wires. Ralph used his big hands to move one box, and Vanellope used her small hands to reach behind a screen. They looked left and right. At last, Vanellope found the button near a cable.

She picked it up and smiled. “We got it.”

Ralph gave the button back to the man in the blue jacket. The man said thank you and gave them two small tickets for a game ride. Ralph laughed. Vanellope laughed too, because the ride was tiny but very fast.

After that, they sat near a glowing sign and ate sweet snacks. Ralph looked at the internet again. It was still loud and wild, but now it felt less scary.

He learned a simple lesson. Big problems can feel smaller when friends stay calm and work together. And sometimes the best way to fix a mess is to slow down and look carefully.
