# The Forgotten Flag

Asha woke up early in Rosas because the day was special. There would be a music walk in the town square that evening, and many families were going to sing together. The streets were already busy with people carrying flowers, food, and bright cloth flags.

Asha wanted to help her grandfather get ready. He had made a small golden flag for the walk, and he was very proud of it.

"Do not let me forget this," he said with a smile.

"I will remember," Asha said.

Later that afternoon, Asha and her goat Valentino carried baskets to the square. Musicians were tuning their instruments, and children were running between the chairs. Everything felt full of life.

Then Asha looked at the baskets and gasped. "The flag!"

She had left it at home.

Her grandfather would be sad, and there was not much time before the music walk began.

"Come on, Valentino," Asha said.

They hurried back through the narrow streets. But when they reached the house, a strong wind pushed the door closed. Inside, the little golden flag was not on the table. It was gone.

Asha looked around quickly. Then she saw a small piece of gold cloth near the window.

"The wind took it outside," she said.

She followed the cloth pieces into the garden. At last she found the flag hanging in a lemon tree.

"Too high," Asha said.

Valentino stood under the branch and stayed very still. Asha climbed carefully onto a low wall, then reached the branch and pulled the flag free.

"We did it!" she said.

They ran back to the square just before the music began. Asha gave the flag to her grandfather, and his face became bright with joy.

"Thank you," he said. "You came back in time."

When the music started, Asha held the flag high and sang with everyone.

That evening, she learned that promises are important, and when you make a mistake, it is best to act quickly and fix it with all your heart.
