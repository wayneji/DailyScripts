# The Route for Everyone

## Part 1: The Fast Test

Lightning McQueen waits outside Flo's cafe in Radiator Springs. Some visitors want to drive around town before lunch. Sally asks him to choose a short route for them.

McQueen puts an orange cone beside the starting point. Then he drives past the shops, around a hill, and back to the cafe.

"Only ten minutes!" he says. "There is plenty of time."

Mater looks at the hill. "Did you stop anywhere?"

"No. I know the road."

"The visitors don't," Mater says. "And some of them drive much more slowly than you."

McQueen looks at the route again. Ten minutes is his time. Perhaps it will not be everyone's time.

## Part 2: A Different Driver

Mater offers to try the route. McQueen follows him and watches carefully.

At the first corner, Mater stops beside two blue flower pots. One pot is in front of the arrow on a low sign. McQueen knows which way to turn, but a new visitor may not.

They ask Flo to move the pot a little. Now the arrow is easy to see.

Next, they reach the hill. Mater can climb it, but he goes slowly and needs a rest at the top.

"There's a flat road beside the park," he says.

McQueen tries it with him. It is longer, but Mater reaches the end without stopping. They decide to use that road instead.

## Part 3: Together at Lunch

Before the visitors arrive, Sally places a white flag at the finish beside the cafe. McQueen waits near the orange cone.

He tells the visitors about the turn and the road by the park. Then he lets a small, slow car lead the group. Mater stays at the back so nobody gets left behind.

The trip takes twenty minutes. Everyone returns together, ready for lunch.

"That was easy to follow," a visitor tells Sally.

McQueen smiles. His first test was faster, but this trip works better.

He thanks Mater for trying the route.

"You just needed a different driver," Mater says.

Next time McQueen plans a trip, he will ask who is coming before he starts his engine.

## Useful A2 Words

- **route**: the roads you follow to get somewhere.
- **arrow**: a mark that shows which way to go.
- **instead**: in place of something else.

## Reading Questions

1. Why does McQueen think again about his ten-minute test?
   - A. He forgets to drive past the shops.
   - B. Sally asks him to make the trip faster.
   - C. Other cars may need more time than he does.

2. Why do they choose the road beside the park?
   - A. It takes McQueen less than ten minutes.
   - B. Mater can use it without needing a rest.
   - C. It has more flower pots for visitors to see.

3. Why does Mater stay at the back of the group?
   - A. To help keep all the visitors together.
   - B. To get to the cafe before the slow car.
   - C. To move the flower pot after the trip.

4. What does McQueen learn about planning a trip?
   - A. A good trip always uses the shortest road.
   - B. Visitors should learn the town before coming.
   - C. A good route needs to suit the people using it.
