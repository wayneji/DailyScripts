# Baymax! S01E01: The Yellow Art Folder

## Part 1: A Call at the Café

Cass sat behind the counter at the Lucky Cat Café with her injured ankle on a stool. Baymax stood beside her and reminded her not to walk unless it was necessary.

The telephone rang. A teacher from the community art club was coming at two o'clock for a yellow folder containing the children's drawings. Cass had promised to keep it safe, but it was not near the cash desk.

“I will find it,” Cass said, starting to stand.

Baymax gently moved in front of her. “Your ankle needs time to recover. You can help me by giving instructions.”

Cass sat down again. She remembered placing the folder near the café window beside four tea tins.

## Part 2: The Paper Stars

Baymax searched near the window. The folder was gone, but a small blue paper star lay on the floor. He found another star near the cake cupboard and a third beside a low supply shelf.

Cass explained that the stars had been inside the folder. “It may have fallen behind that shelf,” she said.

The gap was too narrow for Baymax's hand. He thought about moving the shelf, but a hot tea machine stood on top. That could be dangerous.

Cass studied the café tools and suggested the long wooden serving spoon. Baymax pushed it into the gap and pulled out the folder. One corner was wet because rain had entered through a partly open window, but the drawings were dry.

## Part 3: Helping While Resting

Baymax closed the window and placed the folder on a clean towel. Cass checked every drawing while remaining in her chair. Then Baymax put the folder inside a clear bag to protect it from more rain.

When the teacher arrived, the folder was ready. She thanked them and showed them a cheerful drawing of the café.

Cass smiled. Resting had not stopped her from remembering details, noticing danger, and suggesting the right tool.

“Accepting help is also part of recovering,” Baymax said.

Cass agreed. She kept her ankle on the stool while Baymax moved the four tea tins to a safer, drier place.

## Useful A2 Words

- **recover**: to become well again after being ill or injured.
- **narrow**: having only a small distance from one side to the other.
- **instruction**: information that tells someone what to do.

## Reading Questions

1. Why did Baymax ask Cass to give instructions instead of searching?
   - A. Her injured ankle needed more time to recover.
   - B. She could not remember what the folder looked like.
   - C. The teacher wanted Baymax to work alone.

2. Why did Baymax use the long serving spoon?
   - A. He needed to close the window from across the room.
   - B. His hand could not enter the narrow gap safely.
   - C. The paper stars were stuck to the top of the shelf.

3. What did Cass learn after the teacher arrived?
   - A. Resting does not stop someone from offering useful help.
   - B. Important papers should always be kept beside tea tins.
   - C. A café should close whenever somebody has an injury.

4. What is the main message of the whole story?
   - A. Working quickly is the best way to protect valuable things.
   - B. Careful teamwork can solve a problem without causing more harm.
   - C. People should solve every problem without changing their plans.
