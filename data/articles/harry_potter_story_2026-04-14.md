# A Rainy Day at Hogwarts

Harry Potter and his friend Ron Weasley were in the Gryffindor common room. It was a very rainy Sunday afternoon. The wind was blowing loudly outside the castle walls. Harry and Ron wanted to practice some magic spells for their next class, but they did not have much energy because they were tired after a long week of studying.

"I am bored," Ron said. He looked at his old, broken wand. "Let's try a simple flying spell," he suggested.

Harry agreed. He stood up and took his wand. "Wingardium Leviosa," he said clearly. Suddenly, a heavy book on the table started to shake. But then, it stopped and fell to the floor with a loud noise. They tried again and again, but the book stayed on the floor. 

"Maybe we need more focus," Harry said. He took a deep breath and looked at the book. Ron stood next to him and helped him. They were good friends, so they worked together. They did not get angry even when the spell failed.

After a few minutes, they tried one more time. Harry said the words slowly, and Ron pointed his wand at the book. Suddenly, the book slowly went up into the air. It stayed there for a moment before coming back down gently.

"We did it!" Ron cheered. 

Harry smiled at his friend. He learned that magic is not just about saying the right words. It is also about patience and working together with a friend. Even on a dark and rainy day, a little teamwork made their day bright and happy. They felt proud of their small success and ready for the next day at school.
