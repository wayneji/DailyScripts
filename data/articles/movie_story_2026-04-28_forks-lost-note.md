# Forky's Lost Note

Bonnie was getting ready for school. Her bag was on the chair, her shoes were by the door, and her toys were on the floor. Woody wanted the room to stay calm, but Forky was walking around with a small piece of paper in his hand.

"What is that?" Woody asked.

Forky looked worried. "Bonnie dropped it last night," he said. "I think it is important, but I do not know what it says."

Woody looked at the paper. It had big, careful letters: "Show and Tell - Bring your favorite toy."

Woody opened his eyes wide. "This is for school today! Bonnie needs this note."

Forky felt bad. "I wanted to help, but then I forgot."

Woody put a hand on Forky's shoulder. "It is okay. We still have time, but we must move fast."

Bonnie and her mother were in the kitchen. The bedroom door was half closed. Woody and Forky had to get the note onto Bonnie's school bag.

First, they climbed onto the chair. Then Forky almost fell because the paper covered his face. Woody caught him just in time.

"Thank you," Forky said.

"Careful now," Woody said.

They reached the top of the bag, but a window was open, and a little wind blew the note away. It landed under the bed.

"Oh no!" Forky cried.

They hurried down and crawled under the bed. There was dust, an old sock, and a red crayon. Woody found the note near the wall.

Then they heard Bonnie's steps coming back.

"Quick!" Woody said.

Forky carried one side of the note, and Woody carried the other. Together they pulled it out and pushed it into the front pocket of the school bag.

A moment later, Bonnie came in. She checked her bag and saw the note.

"Good! I almost forgot," she said happily.

Woody and Forky hid behind a box and smiled.

Forky whispered, "I made a mistake, but we fixed it together."

Woody nodded. "That is what friends do."

That morning, they learned that helping is not about being perfect. It is about not giving up.
