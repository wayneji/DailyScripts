# Paddington and the Red Scarf Day

It was a cold day in London, but Paddington was cheerful. He was walking with the Brown family to a little winter market near the river. The stalls were full of warm soup, bright oranges, and shiny toys.

Paddington wore his blue coat and his favorite red scarf. Mrs. Brown said it made him look very smart.

"Thank you," Paddington said. "I do try."

At the market, a little girl began to cry near the sweet shop.

"My scarf is gone," she said. "My mum made it for me, and I cannot find it."

Paddington looked under a bench. He looked behind a box of apples. He looked next to a pile of snow hats. The scarf was not there.

Jonathan pointed up. "Look!"

A small wind had blown the scarf onto the top of a market sign. It was hanging there like a flag.

"Oh dear," Paddington said. "It has gone on a little adventure."

The sign was too high for the girl to reach. Mr. Brown tried to stand on a crate, but it wobbled. Paddington thought for a moment. Then he opened his marmalade sandwich tin and took out a long spoon.

Mrs. Brown blinked. "Paddington?"

Paddington tied his red scarf to the spoon and used it like a soft hook. With one careful pull, the girl's scarf came down safely into his paws.

The girl laughed with joy. "Thank you!"

Paddington bowed politely. "You are welcome."

The girl's mum came back a minute later and hugged her. The Browns smiled, and Paddington bought a small jar of honey for everyone to share.

That evening, Paddington felt very happy. He had helped someone, and the winter market looked warmer than before.

Paddington learned that a small idea, a kind heart, and a little patience can save the day.
