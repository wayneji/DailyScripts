# Zootopia and the Little Lost Bus Pass

It was a busy morning in Zootopia. Cars rolled past, umbrellas moved fast, and the streets were full of happy noise. Judy Hopps was on her way to work when she saw a young fox standing near a bus stop.

The fox looked worried. He had a school bag, a blue cap, and a small lunch box with carrot stickers on it.

"What's wrong?" Judy asked.

"I lost my bus pass," the fox said. "My class starts soon."

Judy looked at the ground. She looked under the bench. She looked near the ticket machine. But the bus pass was not there.

Nick Wilde came walking over with a coffee cup in his hand. "Looks like we have a tiny mystery," he said.

The fox sighed. "My mother gave me the pass this morning. Then I dropped my bag, and now it is gone."

Judy smiled. "Don't worry. We will help."

She asked the bus driver to wait. Nick checked around the kiosk. He looked behind a map board. Then he found the bus pass stuck on the back of a sticky poster.

"Aha!" Nick said. "It was hiding in plain sight."

The fox's ears stood up. "That's mine!"

Judy gave it back. "Next time, keep it in a safe pocket."

The fox nodded quickly. "I will."

Just then, the bus door opened again. The driver smiled. "Lucky for you, there is still time."

The fox hurried onto the bus and waved through the window. Judy waved back. Nick took a sip of coffee and smiled.

"Another great day in Zootopia," he said.

Judy laughed. "And another small problem solved."

That day, the fox learned that even when a morning goes wrong, kind friends can help make it right. And Judy learned that small clues can solve a big worry.
