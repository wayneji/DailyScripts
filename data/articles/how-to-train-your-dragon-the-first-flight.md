# The Dragon's Secret Lesson

Hiccup is a young boy on the island of Berk. All people on the island fight dragons, but Hiccup is different. He wants to understand them. One day, he goes to the woods and finds a Night Fury dragon. The dragon is hurt and cannot fly. Hiccup feels sad for the dragon. He decides to help.

Hiccup gives the dragon some fish and names him Toothless. They spend many days together. Hiccup learns that dragons are not bad; they are just afraid. When Toothless is better, he tries to fly but falls down. Hiccup knows he must make something to help his friend. He works in his shop for many hours. He builds a new tail fin for Toothless.

With the new fin, Toothless can fly again! He is very happy. Hiccup climbs on his back, and they fly together in the sky. It is a wonderful feeling. Hiccup sees the world from high above. He learns that friendship is powerful. 

Hiccup goes back to the village. He tells his friends that dragons are kind. From that day, the people of Berk and the dragons live together in peace. Hiccup learns that being brave is not just about fighting; it is about changing your mind and choosing kindness. He is proud of his dragon friend.
