# Inside Out and the Lost Note

Riley sat in her new room and looked at a small note on her desk. It was a list of things she wanted to do at school. But then the note slipped behind the bed and disappeared.

Inside Headquarters, Joy saw the problem first. “We need that note,” she said. Sadness looked worried, and Fear started to pace in a circle. Anger wanted to shout, but Joy said, “Let’s stay calm and look carefully.”

Joy sent the emotions to search one memory at a time. They checked the desk, the chair, and the floor. Disgust found a pencil, but not the note. Then Sadness noticed a corner of paper under the bed. It was stuck near a sock.

Fear did not want to go in, but he did it anyway. He reached under the bed and pulled the paper out. It was a little wrinkled, but it was the right note.

Riley smiled when she found it. She read the list again and felt better about her day. One small thing had made her nervous, but now it was back in her hands.

Joy learned that looking fast is not always enough. Sadness learned that careful eyes can help too. Riley learned that when a small problem feels big, it helps to stop, breathe, and search step by step.
