# The Quiet Mountain Path

Tian and his mother were walking on a mountain path early in the day. The sky was pale blue, and the clouds moved slowly above the trees. They wanted to bring food to Tian's grandmother, who lived in a small house near the top of the mountain.

Tian carried a basket with rice cakes and fruit. His mother carried tea leaves and warm soup in a round pot. The road was not very hard, but it was long.

"Walk carefully," his mother said. "The path is narrow today because of the rain last night."

Tian nodded. He looked at the wet stones and stepped slowly.

After some time, they reached a place where the path turned around a big rock. There they saw an old man sitting on the ground. His walking stick was broken in two pieces.

"Are you hurt?" Tian asked.

The old man shook his head. "No, but I cannot walk up or down without my stick. The ground is too wet."

Tian looked worried. Grandmother was waiting, but the old man also needed help.

His mother said softly, "We should stop and think."

Tian looked around. Then he saw a long bamboo branch near the side of the path. It was light, but strong. He picked it up and cleaned off the wet leaves.

"Maybe this can help," he said.

His mother used a strip of cloth from the basket cover to tie the broken part of the old man's handle to the bamboo. Together they made a new walking stick.

The old man stood up slowly and tested it. It held his weight well.

He smiled. "This is very kind of you. I can walk again now."

Tian felt happy. "We are going to my grandmother's house," he said. "You can walk with us if you want."

So the three of them went up the mountain together. They moved slowly, but safely. When they arrived, Grandmother was waiting by the door with a warm smile.

That day, Tian learned that reaching your goal is important, but helping someone on the road can make the journey better for everyone.
