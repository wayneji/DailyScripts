# The Secret Song at Sunset

Rumi and Mira lived in a busy city full of music, lights, and secret danger. In the daytime, they practiced songs with their group. At night, they fought demons together. They were brave, but they were also very tired.

One evening, the sky was orange and pink. The group had a show the next day, so Rumi wanted to practice one more song on the roof of their building. Mira came with her and carried two bottles of water.

When they started to sing, they heard a strange sound from the next roof. It was soft at first, then louder. It was not part of their song.

Mira looked surprised. "Do you think it is a demon?" she asked.

"Maybe," Rumi said. "Let us check, but be careful."

They walked slowly to the next roof. Behind a big black water tank, they found a young demon boy. He was thin and shaking. In his hands was a broken music player.

The boy looked scared. "Please do not fight me," he said. "I do not want to hurt anyone. I only wanted to hear music."

Rumi and Mira looked at each other. They knew demons could be dangerous, but this boy looked sad, not angry.

"Why are you here?" Mira asked.

The boy looked down. "I am alone," he said. "Your songs make me feel calm. This player is broken, so I came here to listen."

Rumi felt sorry for him. She sat down on the roof. "Music can help many hearts," she said.

Mira had an idea. She took out a small tool from her bag and fixed the music player. Then Rumi sang a quiet song while the city lights came on.

The demon boy smiled for the first time. "Thank you," he said. "I will go now. I will remember your kindness."

After that, he left peacefully. Rumi and Mira watched the sunset and finished their practice.

The next day, they sang with strong hearts. They learned that night that kindness is not weakness. Sometimes it is the best way to stop fear.
