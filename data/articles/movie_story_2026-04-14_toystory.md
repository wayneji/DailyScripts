# Toy Story: Andy's New Game

One sunny afternoon, Andy was playing in his room. He took out all his favorite toys, including Woody, Buzz Lightyear, and the others. Today, Andy wanted to play a new game. He lined up all the toys on the floor and put on his favorite music.

"Okay, toys," Andy said with a big smile. "Today, we are going on a big adventure!"

Woody looked at Buzz. "What is Andy planning now?" Woody asked.

"I do not know," Buzz said. "But we always have fun when he plays with us."

Andy put a small cardboard box on the floor. He pretended it was a rocket ship. He took Woody and Buzz and put them inside the box. "Blast off!" Andy shouted. The toys shook and moved around inside the box. They laughed and held on to each other.

After a while, Andy opened the box. "The adventure is over for today," he said. He put the toys back on the shelf. But when he did, Woody fell and landed behind the bed. Nobody saw him.

Later, when Andy left the room, Woody called out. "Help! I am behind the bed!"

Buzz looked down and saw Woody. He jumped off the shelf and slid down to help his friend. Together, they pushed and pulled until Woody was back on the shelf.

"Thank you, Buzz," Woody said. "You are a true friend."

Buzz smiled. "That is what friends do. We always help each other."

They waited happily on the shelf for the next time Andy wanted to play. They knew that no matter what happened, their friendship was strong and would last forever.
