# The Red Lantern Day

Mei was at school on a bright spring morning. The hallway was busy, and students were talking about the temple fair after class. Mei wanted to go with her friends, because there would be music, food, and paper lanterns.

Her best friend Priya was excited too. She held a small red lantern she had made in art class. It had gold stars on it, and it looked very nice.

"I hope it does not break," Priya said.

"It is strong," Mei said. "We can carry it carefully."

After lunch, Mei and Priya walked to the school yard. The sky was clear, but the wind was stronger than before. They were careful, yet a sudden gust pushed the lantern hard. The string slipped from Priya's hand, and the lantern rolled under a bench near the basketball court.

Priya looked sad. "Oh no. It is bent now. We worked so hard on it."

Mei knelt down and picked it up. The paper was torn a little, and one gold star was folded, but the star shape was still there. Mei shook the dust off the bench, and Priya held the lantern close so it would not tear more.

"We can fix it," Mei said.

They went to the art room and found glue, tape, and a piece of red paper. Mei held the lantern open while Priya placed the new paper inside. Then they used a thin ribbon to make the top stronger.

When they finished, the lantern looked different, but it was still beautiful.

Priya smiled. "It is even better now."

At the temple fair, they hung the lantern near the stage. The light from inside made the red paper shine softly in the dark evening.

Mei felt happy. She had worried about the broken lantern, but helping Priya had made the day better.

That night, Mei learned that when something small breaks, kindness and teamwork can make it shine again.
