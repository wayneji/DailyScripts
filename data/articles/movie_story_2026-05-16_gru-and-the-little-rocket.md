# Gru and the Little Rocket

It was a sunny morning in Gru’s house. The kitchen was full of soft light, and the minions were busy in the yard. Agnes stood near the door with a small smile. It was her birthday, and Gru wanted to give her a special gift.

He had made a little rocket toy from wood, silver paint, and red paper. It was not big, but it looked bright and fun. Agnes clapped her hands when she saw it.

"Can I try it?" she asked.

"Of course," Gru said.

He put the rocket on the path in the yard. The toy had small wheels, and it could roll when you pushed it. Agnes gave it a gentle push, but one wheel was loose. The rocket turned too fast, rolled into a flower bed, and stopped under a bush.

Agnes looked sad. "Oh no. Did I break it?"

Gru knelt down and looked at the toy. The wheel was not broken, but the little screw had fallen out. He sighed. He wanted the gift to be perfect, but he also knew Agnes felt bad.

"No problem," he said. "We can fix it together."

He went to the garage and brought a tiny screwdriver, a spare screw, and a little box of tools. Agnes held the rocket while Gru repaired the wheel. Then he showed her how to check it by turning it slowly.

After that, the rocket rolled straight across the yard. The minions cheered and jumped up and down. Agnes laughed and ran after it.

Gru smiled. The gift was simple, but the best part was not the toy. It was the time they shared.

That evening, Agnes put the rocket on her shelf and said, "I like it because we made it work together."

Gru nodded. He learned that a good gift is not only something new. It is also care, patience, and love.
