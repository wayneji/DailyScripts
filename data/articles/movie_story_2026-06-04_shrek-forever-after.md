## Part 1: A Busy Morning in the Swamp

The swamp was loud that morning. Birds sang in the trees, the water moved softly, and Donkey talked without stopping.

"This picnic will be amazing!" Donkey said. "We need soft bread, sweet juice, big apples, little cakes, and maybe ten songs."

Shrek sighed. "A picnic needs food, not ten songs."

Fiona laughed as she packed a basket near the table outside their house. The children were playing with wooden frogs and running in the mud.

"The picnic is for everyone," Fiona said. "Even you can smile for one afternoon."

Shrek lifted the basket with one hand. It was heavy, but that did not surprise him. What did surprise him was the missing pie.

"Where is the onion pie?" he asked.

Donkey stopped talking for one second. "It was right here. I saw it. I even smelled it. It smelled like a happy onion."

Fiona looked around the yard. There were crumbs on the table, a green leaf on the ground, and a tiny line of mud going toward the forest path.

"That is strange," she said.

Puss in Boots jumped lightly onto the fence. "A missing pie is serious business," he said. "I will help."

Shrek pointed at the muddy line. "Good. We follow that."

Donkey looked worried. "What if a giant pie-eating monster lives in the woods?"

Shrek started walking. "Then we ask for our pie back."

They followed the path away from the swamp. Soon they found more clues: one empty berry cup, a bent flower, and a piece of blue ribbon on a bush.

Fiona picked up the ribbon. "This looks like it came from Far Far Away."

"So our pie thief has style," Donkey said.

At the end of the path, they could see the old stone gate near the road to the town.

## Part 2: The Hungry Gate Guard

When they reached the gate, they did not find a monster. They found a young gate guard sitting on a box with the onion pie on his knees.

He looked scared when he saw Shrek.

"Please do not be angry," the guard said quickly. "My name is Tom. I was on duty since sunrise. I forgot my lunch, and the smell of this pie was too wonderful."

Donkey opened his mouth wide. "You stole a family picnic pie because you were hungry?"

Tom looked down. "I only took one piece first. But then it was so good. And then I heard your steps, and I did not know what to do."

Puss put his paws on his hips. "You could begin with the truth."

Fiona looked at the boy's face and the empty cup beside him. "Have you eaten anything else today?"

Tom shook his head. "Only two berries. My mother is sick, and I had to start work early."

Shrek folded his arms. He was upset, but he also remembered many hungry days from long ago.

"Stealing is still wrong," Shrek said. "But sitting here hungry is wrong too."

Donkey sniffed the pie. "There is enough left for sharing. Also, I packed extra carrots. I do not know why. I just felt smart."

Shrek gave him a slow smile. "That may be the smartest thing you said all day."

Tom looked surprised. "You will still have your picnic?"

"Yes," Fiona said, "and first we make sure you eat properly."

They spread a cloth under a tree beside the gate. Donkey poured juice. Puss carried plates with serious care. Shrek cut the pie into equal pieces.

Tom took one bite and closed his eyes. "This is the best pie in the kingdom."

## Part 3: A Better Picnic

While they ate, Tom told them more. The king's road had been busy all week, and the guards had not had enough help. Some workers missed meals because they were trying to do too much.

Fiona listened carefully. "Then this is not only about one pie. It is a bigger problem."

Puss cleaned his whiskers. "A hungry worker makes poor choices."

Donkey nodded. "Also, a hungry donkey sings very sad songs."

Shrek stood up and looked at the road, the gate, and the tired young guard. "We can fix this part today."

He sent Donkey back to the swamp with a note for the others. Soon the picnic became much larger. Fiona's basket arrived. More bread came from town. Even the blacksmith sent hot soup in a pot.

By afternoon, the gate had turned into a small rest place for travelers and workers. People sat in the shade and shared simple food. Tom helped serve everyone and thanked each person with a red face and a shy smile.

"I will pay you back for the pie," he told Shrek.

Shrek shook his head. "Help the next hungry person. That is enough."

Before the family went home, Tom tied a new blue ribbon to the gate.

"So I remember this day," he said.

Donkey looked at the ribbon and smiled. "Good. And next time, ask before you eat the onion pie."

Tom laughed. "I promise."

As the sun went down, Shrek, Fiona, Donkey, and Puss walked back through the warm evening light. The picnic had not happened in the quiet swamp they planned, but it had become something better.

Fiona touched Shrek's arm. "You turned a bad surprise into a kind day."

Shrek looked at the empty basket and then at his family. "Do not tell anyone," he said, "but this picnic was not terrible."

Donkey jumped happily. "I knew it! Group picnics and eleven songs next time!"

Shrek groaned, and everyone laughed all the way home.

## Useful A2 Words

- **crumbs**: very small pieces of bread or cake.
- **guard**: a person who watches and protects a place.
- **shade**: a cool dark place away from the sun.
