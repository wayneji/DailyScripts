# Zootopia and the Lost Bus Pass

Judy Hopps woke up early on a bright morning in Zootopia. She had one small job to do before work. She wanted to take the city bus to the police station because her bike had a flat tire.

She reached into her bag and stopped. Her bus pass was gone.

“Oh no,” Judy said. “I need that pass today.”

Nick Wilde was waiting near the bus stop with a coffee in his hand. He saw Judy looking in every pocket.

“Lost something?” he asked.

“My bus pass,” Judy said. “I had it last night.”

Nick smiled. “Then let’s think like detectives.”

First, they checked Judy’s bag again. Then they looked under the bench, behind the sign, and near the flower box. Nothing was there. The bus came and went, and Judy felt worried.

“I am late,” she said. “And I do not want to be late on my first case of the day.”

Nick looked around carefully. Then he pointed to a little rabbit-shaped sticker on the ground.

“Look,” he said. “Your pass may have slipped out when you took your keys.”

They followed the small trail of stickers to a snack stand next to the stop. There, under a box of carrots, was the bus pass.

Judy picked it up and laughed. “It was hiding right in front of me.”

The snack seller came out and said, “I found it when I swept the floor. I was going to keep it safe.”

“Thank you,” Judy said.

Nick waved at the next bus. “You still have time.”

Judy got on the bus and sat by the window. She felt much better.

That day, she learned that a calm mind and a good friend can solve a small problem before it becomes a big one.
