# Joy and the Quiet Light

Inside Riley’s mind, Joy was busy in Headquarters. Bright screens glowed on the walls, and memory balls rolled on the floor. It was a normal afternoon, and Joy wanted everything to feel happy and safe.

Then she saw a small blue light near the back of the room. It was a new memory ball, and it was moving slowly under a chair.

"Oh no," Joy said. "It is going away."

Sadness came closer and looked at it. "Maybe it is lost because it wants help," she said softly.

Joy did not want a slow memory ball. She liked bright yellow ones. She tried to pick it up fast, but the ball rolled into a dark corner and stopped behind some boxes.

Now the room felt quiet. Joy looked around. She wanted to fix the problem alone, but the path was too small, and the boxes were too heavy.

Sadness pointed with one finger. "We can move them together."

Joy nodded. She pulled one box, and Sadness pushed another. They worked slowly, but they made space. Then Joy reached the blue memory ball and held it in her hand.

It was not a happy memory. It was a memory of Riley feeling shy on her first day at a new school. Joy wanted to put it away, but Sadness said, "Wait. Look again."

Joy looked at the memory ball. She saw Riley’s face, a little worried at first, but then a classmate smiled and said hello. The memory was hard at the start, but it was also important. It showed that Riley could be brave.

Joy smiled a small smile. "Maybe this memory is good after all."

She placed the ball in a safe place with the other memories. Then the room felt calm, not loud, but warm.

That night, Joy learned something new. Happiness is important, but quiet feelings can help too. Sometimes a sad moment can carry hope inside it. And when friends work together, even a lost light can find its way home.
