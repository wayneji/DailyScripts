# Finding Nemo and the Blue Shell Map

Nemo was playing near the coral with his friends when he found a small blue shell. It was smooth and shiny, and it had three white lines across the top.

"It looks like a map," Nemo said.

Dory swam closer and smiled. "A map to somewhere exciting?"

Nemo turned the shell around. The white lines looked like waves, and one tiny mark looked like a star. He felt curious, but also a little nervous. His dad, Marlin, always said the reef was safe and the open water was not for games.

Just then, a baby sea turtle swam past very fast.

"Has anyone seen my snack basket?" the turtle asked. "It floated away!"

Nemo looked again at the blue shell. "Maybe this can help."

The friends followed the first white line around a tall coral tower. Then they followed the second line past a patch of soft green sea grass. Dory hummed a happy tune, but Nemo kept checking that they were not going too far.

At last, the third line led them to a quiet rock pool. There, beside a pink coral branch, was the turtle's little basket. A tiny crab was sitting inside it.

"This is not your house," Nemo said kindly.

The crab clicked its claws and climbed out. Nemo pushed the basket back to the turtle.

"You found it!" the turtle cheered.

Marlin arrived a moment later, looking worried. Nemo showed him the shell and explained the map.

Marlin took a deep breath. "You stayed near the reef and helped a friend. That was brave and careful."

Nemo smiled. The blue shell was not a map to treasure, but it had led them to something better: a chance to help.
