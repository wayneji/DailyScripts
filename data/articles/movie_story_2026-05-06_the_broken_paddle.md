# The Broken Paddle

Lilo and Nani went to the beach early in the morning. The sky was clear, and the water was bright blue. Stitch ran behind them with a towel on his head and a big smile on his face. Nani wanted a calm morning before work, so she brought a small boat to the water.

"Only a short ride," Nani said.

Lilo nodded, and Stitch jumped into the boat first.

They moved across the quiet sea, and everything felt peaceful. Lilo sang a little song, and Stitch put his hand in the water and laughed. But after some time, one paddle made a loud crack.

"Oh no," Nani said. "The paddle is broken."

The boat turned slowly to one side. They were not far from shore, but the wind was getting stronger.

Lilo looked worried. "Can we still go back?"

Nani tried to stay calm. "Yes, but we must think first."

Stitch picked up the broken paddle and looked at it. Then he looked at the towel on his head and had an idea. He tied the towel tightly around the broken part of the paddle.

"That will not be strong enough," Nani said.

Stitch shook his head and pointed at a flat board in the bottom of the boat. It was under a fishing box. Nani understood. Together, they tied the small board to the paddle with the towel.

Now the paddle was longer and stronger.

"Good thinking, Stitch!" Lilo said.

Nani used the fixed paddle carefully, and Stitch used his hands to help move the boat. Slowly, they turned back toward the beach.

A few waves hit the boat, but they stayed calm and worked together. Soon they could see the sand more clearly. Then they reached the shore and pulled the boat onto the beach.

Lilo hugged Stitch. "You saved our morning," she said.

Stitch smiled proudly. Nani laughed and touched his head. "Family helps family," she said.

Then they sat on the sand and watched the sea.

That morning, they learned that a broken thing does not always end the journey. With calm hearts and teamwork, people can find a way home.
