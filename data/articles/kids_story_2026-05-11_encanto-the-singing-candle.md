# Encanto and the Singing Candle

Mirabel woke up to a soft sound in Casita. It was not the floor tiles. It was not the windows. It was a tiny song coming from the kitchen table.

On the table stood a small candle. Its light moved gently, and the flame seemed to sing, "La, la, la."

"That is new," Mirabel said.

Antonio came in with a little bird on his shoulder. "The bird says the candle is nervous."

"A candle can be nervous?" Mirabel asked.

Antonio nodded. "This one can."

Today was family music day. Everyone was going to sing in the courtyard. The small candle had to stand near the flowers, but it was afraid its light was not bright enough.

Mirabel picked up the candle carefully. "You do not need to be the biggest light," she said. "You just need to be your own light."

The candle sang a very small note. "La."

In the courtyard, Luisa moved chairs. Isabela made flowers. Camilo made funny faces to help the children laugh.

When the music began, the small candle shook. Its song became quiet.

Mirabel stood beside it and started to clap slowly. Antonio clapped too. Soon the whole family clapped a soft beat.

The candle listened. Then it sang again, a little louder.

"La, la, la!"

Its light danced over the flowers. The children smiled and sang with it. The song was simple, but it made everyone feel happy.

After the music, Mirabel put the candle back on the table.

"You were wonderful," she said.

The candle gave one happy little glow.

Mirabel learned that you do not have to be loud or perfect to bring joy. A brave small song can fill a whole house with light.
