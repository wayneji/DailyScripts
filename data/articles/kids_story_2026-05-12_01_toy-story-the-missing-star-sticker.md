# Toy Story and the Missing Star Sticker

Andy had a bright yellow star sticker on his school notebook. He liked it very much because his teacher gave it to him after he helped clean the classroom. That afternoon, he put the notebook on his desk and ran outside to play.

When the room became quiet, Woody climbed down from the bed and looked at the desk.

"That star looks important," he said.

Buzz Lightyear nodded. "A star is a serious space symbol."

Just then, a small wind came through the open window. The notebook pages moved, and the yellow star sticker flew off. It spun in the air like a tiny kite and landed somewhere behind the toy box.

"Oh no," Woody said. "Andy will be upset if he cannot find it."

The toys began to search. Jessie checked under the chair. Rex looked behind the books, but he was so nervous that he bumped his tail on the wall. Buzz used his shiny helmet to catch the light.

"There!" Buzz said.

The sticker was stuck to a red toy car. The car was under the toy box, and the space was too small for Woody to reach.

Slinky had an idea. "I can help."

He stretched carefully under the toy box. Woody held one end, and Jessie held the other. Slinky pushed the car gently until it rolled out.

The yellow star was safe.

Woody placed it back on Andy's notebook and pressed it down with both hands. The toys hurried back to their places just before Andy came into the room.

Andy saw the star and smiled.

That evening, Woody felt proud. The toys had worked together, and a small sticker had made everyone feel like a hero.
