# Audio Script: Finding Nemo and the Blue Shell Map

Host 1: Good morning, reef explorers! Today we are diving into a bright little Finding Nemo adventure, where one small blue shell starts a very curious search.

Host 2: Ooh, a shell mystery before breakfast. That sounds much better than losing a sock, unless the sock is also underwater, which would be very soggy.

Host 1: Our story begins near the coral, where Nemo is playing with his friends. He finds a small blue shell that is smooth, shiny, and marked with three white lines.

Host 2: Nemo looks at it and says it looks like a map. That is a brilliant sentence, because once something looks like a map, your brain immediately wants to follow it.

Host 1: Dory swims closer and asks if it might be a map to somewhere exciting. She sounds cheerful, as usual, and Nemo feels curious.

Host 2: But he also feels a little nervous. His dad, Marlin, has taught him that the reef is safe, and the open water is not a place for silly games.

Host 1: That is important, because this is not a wild, reckless adventure. Nemo wants to explore, but he is also trying to be careful.

Host 2: Then the story gives them a real reason to move. A baby sea turtle rushes past and asks if anyone has seen a missing snack basket.

Host 1: Wait, what? A snack basket has floated away? That is a small emergency, especially if you are hungry and expecting lunch.

Host 2: Nemo looks again at the blue shell and thinks it might help. So the shell is no longer just interesting; it becomes a possible clue.

Host 1: The friends follow the first white line around a tall coral tower. It is easy to picture them swimming carefully past bright colours and quiet corners.

Host 2: Then they follow the second white line past soft green sea grass. Dory hums a happy tune, which feels very Dory, but Nemo keeps checking where they are.

Host 1: I like that detail. Nemo is curious, but he is not forgetting Marlin's warning. He is watching the reef and making sure they do not go too far.

Host 2: That makes the story feel safe for young listeners too. It says adventure can be fun, but careful choices matter.

Host 1: At last, the third white line leads them to a quiet rock pool. That is where the mystery starts to feel close.

Host 2: And there, beside a pink coral branch, they find the little basket. But it is not empty.

Host 1: A tiny crab is sitting inside it. No way! The missing snack basket has accidentally become a crab's temporary home.

Host 2: Nemo does not shout or grab. He says kindly, "This is not your house," which is a wonderfully polite way to handle a crab problem.

Host 1: The crab clicks its claws and climbs out. Then Nemo pushes the basket back to the baby sea turtle.

Host 2: The turtle cheers, "You found it!" That is the happy moment when the search turns into a success.

Host 1: Then Marlin arrives, and he is worried. That makes sense, because Marlin cares about Nemo and wants him to stay safe near the reef.

Host 2: Nemo shows him the blue shell and explains the map. He is not hiding what happened, which is another careful and honest choice.

Host 1: Marlin takes a deep breath. He tells Nemo that he stayed near the reef and helped a friend, and that this was brave and careful.

Host 2: That is a lovely mix of praise. Brave is good, but brave and careful together is even better.

Host 1: The blue shell was not a map to treasure. There was no chest of gold, no magic crown, and no giant surprise.

Host 2: Instead, it led Nemo to something better: a chance to help someone. That is a quieter kind of treasure, but a very good one.

Host 1: The story has three bright clues: the blue shell, the white lines, and the tiny star mark. They make the search feel playful without making it too complicated.

Host 2: And the places are easy to follow: the coral tower, the green sea grass, and the quiet rock pool. It feels like a little route children can imagine.

Host 1: Nemo grows in a small way too. He is not trying to prove he can do everything alone; he listens to what he knows, checks where he is, and helps.

Host 2: Dory adds warmth with her happy tune, and Marlin brings the safe feeling at the end. Everyone has a clear part in the adventure.

Host 1: So today's takeaway is simple: curiosity is best when it swims beside care.

Host 2: And when you follow clues with a kind heart, you might not find treasure, but you may find a friend who needs help.
