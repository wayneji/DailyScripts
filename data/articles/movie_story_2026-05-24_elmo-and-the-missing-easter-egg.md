# Detective Elmo and the Missing Easter Egg

It was a bright morning on Sesame Street. Flowers sat in the windows, and small paper eggs hung on the lamps. Elmo was very excited, because the street was ready for the Easter party. He wore a small hat and carried a toy magnifying glass. Today, Elmo was a detective.

Then Maria came running to him. “Elmo, the big golden Easter egg is missing!” she said. “We need it for the party basket.”

Elmo’s eyes got big. “Oh no! Elmo will find clues!”

He looked near the playground first. On the grass, he saw a line of shiny yellow crumbs. “A clue!” Elmo said. He followed the crumbs to the cookie shop. There he found Cookie Monster, who had crumbs on his shirt.

“Did you take the egg?” Elmo asked.

Cookie Monster shook his head. “Me did not take egg. Me only take cookies.”

Elmo looked around the shop. On the floor he saw a small blue ribbon. It was tied to a toy wagon. The wagon rolled slowly toward the park.

Elmo followed the wagon and found Big Bird. Big Bird was carrying a basket and smiling.

“Big Bird, do you know about the missing egg?” Elmo asked.

Big Bird pointed to the basket. “Look inside!”

Elmo peeked in and laughed. The big golden Easter egg was there. Big Bird had moved it so the sun would not make it hot. He wanted to keep it safe for the party.

Maria clapped her hands. “You found it!”

Elmo smiled. “Yes! Elmo used clues, and Elmo asked kind questions too.”

Soon the party began. Children played games, and friends shared cake and juice. The golden egg shined in the middle of the basket, and everyone cheered.

Elmo learned a simple lesson that day: when something is missing, stay calm, look carefully, and ask for help. Good clues can lead to a happy ending.
