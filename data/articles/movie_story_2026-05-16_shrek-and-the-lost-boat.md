# Shrek and the Lost Boat

One quiet morning, Shrek walked through the swamp with Donkey. The water was still, and the trees looked green and soft in the sun. Shrek wanted a calm day because Fiona was coming home later, and he wanted to make the swamp look nice.

"I like calm days," said Shrek.

"I like snack days," said Donkey.

Shrek smiled a little. He had a small wooden boat near the edge of the water. It was not a fancy boat, but it was useful. He used it to carry plants and sacks across the swamp.

Then he saw something strange. The boat was gone.

Shrek stopped and looked around. "Where is my boat?"

Donkey pointed to the river. "Uh, maybe it went for a walk?"

Shrek groaned. "Boats do not walk."

They followed the muddy bank and found small tracks in the soft ground. The tracks went to a tree with low branches. Up in the branches, the boat was stuck. A strong wind had blown it there the night before.

Shrek crossed his arms. "Great. Now I need a ladder."

Donkey looked at the tree, then at the swamp. "Or we can use teamwork. I know teamwork. It is like arguing, but with more moving parts."

Shrek laughed, even though he did not want to. He found a long rope and a big log. Donkey pulled the rope while Shrek pushed the log into the mud. Little by little, they made a safe path to the tree.

Shrek climbed up slowly, took the boat, and came back down. It was dirty, but safe.

After that, Shrek and Donkey cleaned the boat together. They also put a small red flag on it so they could find it more easily next time.

When Fiona came home, the swamp looked tidy, and the boat was ready.

That day, Shrek learned that even a small problem feels easier when a friend helps you.
