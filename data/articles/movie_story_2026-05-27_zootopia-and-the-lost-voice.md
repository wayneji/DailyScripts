# Zootopia and the Lost Voice

Judy Hopps woke up early in Zootopia. The streets were still quiet, and the city lights were soft and blue. She had a small job for the morning. A young rabbit from the market had lost her voice after a long day of shouting at customers. She could only whisper, and she was sad because she could not tell people what she needed.

Nick Wilde came with Judy. He carried a bag with water, honey, and a notebook. “We can help,” he said. “We just need to stay calm.”

First, they went to the market stall. The rabbit owner pointed at a list of goods, but the list was old and messy. Judy looked around and asked the nearby sellers if they had seen the missing sound box that the rabbit used to call for help. Nick watched the ground and found small tracks near a crate.

The tracks led behind the fruit stand. There, under a pile of orange boxes, they found the little sound box. Judy picked it up, but it was dusty and broken. The rabbit looked even more worried.

Nick had an idea. He fixed the loose wire with a piece of thin string, and Judy added fresh batteries from the market office. Then the rabbit tried it.

“Hello!” she said, and her voice was back.

The rabbit laughed, and then she cried a little too. She thanked Judy and Nick again and again. The market people smiled, because now the morning could continue in a happy way.

Later, Judy and Nick sat near a fountain and shared hot tea. Judy smiled. “We did not need a big chase today.”

Nick nodded. “No. Sometimes the small problem needs the kindest answer.”

Judy learned that patience can solve a hard day. She also learned that helping one small voice can make a whole city feel brighter.
