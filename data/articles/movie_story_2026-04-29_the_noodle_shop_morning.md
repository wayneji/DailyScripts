# The Noodle Shop Morning

Po woke up early in the Valley of Peace. The sun was just coming up, and the air was cool and fresh. Usually, Po liked slow mornings with a big breakfast, but today he wanted to help at his father's noodle shop before training.

"Good morning, Dad!" Po said as he walked inside.

Mr. Ping looked happy. "That is nice to hear," he said. "We have many guests today, so I can use your help."

Po put on a clean apron and started to carry bowls, vegetables, and soup pots. At first, everything went well. Then Mr. Ping opened a small box and made a worried face.

"Oh no," he said. "The special spice bag is gone."

Po stopped moving. "The special spice bag?"

"Yes," Mr. Ping said. "It gives our morning soup its best taste. Without it, the soup will be too plain."

Po wanted to solve the problem quickly. He looked under the table, behind the chairs, and near the stove. But the spice bag was nowhere.

Then he saw tiny white powder on the floor. It made a little line from the kitchen door to the back room.

"Dad, look!" Po said.

They followed the line together. In the back room, they found Zhen sitting near a basket of apples. She was holding the spice bag and sneezing.

"I am sorry," Zhen said. "I wanted to smell it because it was interesting. Then I sneezed, and I dropped it back here."

Po laughed softly. "So that is our mystery."

Mr. Ping checked the bag. It was open, but there was still enough spice inside.

"We can still use this," he said with a smile.

Po carefully carried the bag to the kitchen. This time, he put it high on a safe shelf. Then Mr. Ping made the soup, and soon the shop was full of warm smells and happy guests.

After the busy morning, Po sat down and drank a bowl of soup.

"Today I learned something," he said. "Big heroes must also be careful with small things."

Mr. Ping smiled. "Yes," he said. "A good day often depends on simple care."

Po nodded. The soup tasted perfect, and the morning felt even better because they solved the problem together.
